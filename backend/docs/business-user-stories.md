# ViziCheck Business User Stories

## Visitor Stories

### Story 1

As a Visitor,

I want to register my profile,

So that I can request visits to organizations.

---

### Story 2

As a Visitor,

I want to view available time slots,

So that I can schedule a visit conveniently.

---

### Story 3

As a Visitor,

I want to receive a QR pass,

So that I can enter the facility quickly.

---

## Tenant Admin Stories

### Story 4

As a Tenant Admin,

I want to publish availability slots,

So that visitors can schedule appointments.

---

### Story 5

As a Tenant Admin,

I want to approve or reject requests,

So that only authorized visitors can enter.

---

### Story 6

As a Tenant Admin,

I want to track visitor history,

So that I can monitor facility visits.

---

## Security Officer Stories

### Story 7

As a Security Officer,

I want to scan visitor QR codes,

So that I can validate visitor identity.

---

### Story 8

As a Security Officer,

I want to check visitors in and out,

So that visitor movement is tracked accurately.

---

### Story 9

As a Security Officer,

I want to see active visitors,

So that I know who is currently inside the premises.

---

## Super Admin Stories

### Story 10

As a Super Admin,

I want to manage tenants and users,

So that the platform remains organized.

---

### Story 11

As a Super Admin,

I want to view audit logs,

So that all activities remain traceable.

---

### Story 12

As a Super Admin,

I want to generate reports,

So that I can analyze system usage and performance.
